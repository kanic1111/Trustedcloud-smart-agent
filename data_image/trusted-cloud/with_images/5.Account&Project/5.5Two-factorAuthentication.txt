# Two-factor Authentication

* Click on the username in the top right corner, then select **Two-factor authentication settings**.
* Click on the username in the upper right corner, then click Change Password.
* Click **Enable** or **Close**, then click **OK**.
* After enabling two-factor authentication, you will need to enter your password and a verification code sent to your mobile device each time you log in.

（Image:./trusted-cloud/image/5.Account&Project/5.Account&Project_5.5Two-factorAuthentication_img1.png）

## Enabling two-factor authentication settings
1. Please download and install the authenticator app on your mobile phone. After the installation is complete, please click **NEXT**.

（Image:./trusted-cloud/image/5.Account&Project/5.Account&Project_5.5Two-factorAuthentication_img2.png）


2. Open the Authenticator app on your mobile phone and scan the QR Code or enter the setup key below.
3. Enter the verification code generated by the Authenticator app and tap **ENABLE VERIFICATION**.

（Image:./trusted-cloud/image/5.Account&Project/5.Account&Project_5.5Two-factorAuthentication_img3.png）

* The **Two-factor authentication activated** window appears, indicating that you have successfully completed and enabled two-factor authentication!
